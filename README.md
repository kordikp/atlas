# RecSys Atlas

Twenty years of ACM RecSys research on one map: 2,212 papers 2007–2025 (Semantic Scholar) and the 263 accepted for RecSys 2026, grouped into 34 regions by the similarity of their titles and abstracts. Filter by author, keyword or years, see measured trends, open the 28 open problems drafted from the RecSys 2026 industry and perspective papers, and answer the questions the Atlas has for this year's authors (answers arrive as GitHub issues in this repository).

Live: https://kordikp.github.io/atlas/ · Paper details and recommendations: https://recsys-atlas-explore.vercel.app

Open problems and author questions are drafted by AI models from abstracts and not yet checked by people. Initiated by Pavel Kordík (FIT CTU Prague & Recombee). Content CC BY 4.0.
