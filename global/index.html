    Recommender-systems research beyond one conference

    Every dot is a paper; nearby papers are about similar things. Dots with a dark ring are ACM RecSys conference papers; the others come from other conferences, journals and preprints. The map is loading.

    Papers: all 2,467 ACM RecSys papers of the ACM RecSys atlas (2007–2026) plus 9,543 recommender-systems works from other venues, selected from 70,381 relevant OpenAlex works (1990–2026): every other work with at least 3.7 citations per year since publication and, for 2023–2026, also the top 12% of each year by citations per year. Relevant means the title is close to the ACM RecSys papers (sentence embeddings). ACM RecSys papers keep their atlas position; every other work sits on the island of the RecSys papers most similar to its title. Industry: at least one author with a company affiliation in OpenAlex or a known industry lab; 80.5% of the relevant works carry any affiliation, and industry shares are computed over those. Regions and subtopics are those of the ACM RecSys atlas. Open problems and their rechecks are drafted by AI models and not yet checked by people. Keyword filters match titles and authors.

    Discussion: RecSys Atlas colab (sign in with GitHub). Paper details and recommendations for ACM RecSys papers: RecSys Atlas Explore (powered by Recombee). Momentum: citations per year from OpenAlex (counts_by_year, fetched 2026-10-01) for 11,821 of the shown papers; 2026 counts through September. Rising = the 2025–26 rate is at least 80% of the paper's best year, that year is 2024 or later and it gets at least 5 citations a year; past peak = the best year is 2023 or earlier and the 2025–26 rate is below half of it; steady = the rest. OpenAlex counts for the latest year are still growing, so the recent rates are low estimates. Data: OpenAlex (CC0), snapshot 2026-09-28. Initiated by Pavel Kordík (FIT CTU Prague & Recombee).
